Official Whitepaper – BPY Token

BEP-20 Token for Governance and Community Utility

1. Executive Summary

BPY is a BEP-20 token deployed on the BNB Smart Chain designed with a 100% community-driven approach and a Fair Launch model. Its purpose is to serve as a tool for decentralized governance and access to Web3 utilities, including DAO voting, community incentive programs, and ecosystem growth.

Important Clarification: BPY is not a payment token, it is not a wallet, nor a financial transfer system, and it holds no relationship, affiliation, or commercial link with the U.S.-based company BitPay Inc.

2. Tokenomics

The project utilizes a transparent structure based on the bonding curve model through Four.meme, ensuring an equitable distribution from the very first block.

Creator Strategic Reserve 200,000,000 20.00% Initial allocation acquired through the Create & Buy feature dedicated to development, marketing, and future liquidity.

Transparency Note (September 2026): The total supply is fully standardized at 1,000,000,000 BPY tokens. Upon reaching the bonding curve target on Four.meme, the accumulated liquidity is automatically deposited and locked on PancakeSwap to ensure market stability.

3. Project Roadmap

Stage 1: Architecture & Setup (Completed)

  • Structuring of the 1,000,000,000 BPY fixed supply model on BNB Chain.
  • Design of the 20% strategic reserve allocation and separation of operational wallets.
  • Publication of core technical infrastructure and governance guidelines on GitHub.

Stage 2: Launch & Capitalization (In Progress)

  • Fair launch deployment via the bonding curve system on the Four.meme platform.
  • Transparent acquisition of the strategic reserve (200M BPY) at the creation block.
  • Automatic token graduation and initial liquidity injection on PancakeSwap.

Stage 3: Ecosystem Expansion

  • Token consolidation and visibility management across community indexers.
  • Evaluation of utility integrations with management tools and Web3 partner platforms.
  • Progressive rollout of the DAO voting portal for community governance decisions.
