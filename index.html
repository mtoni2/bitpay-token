What is BitPay (BPY)?

BitPay (BPY) is a community token deployed on the BNB Chain, engineered under a 100% Fair Launch framework to ensure absolute transparency, equity, and decentralized community participation from genesis. By neutralizing traditional funding frictions, it establishes a reliable infrastructure for distributed Web3 environments.

Purpose and Utility

Serving as the core asset for governance and ecosystem rewards, BPY enables the community to actively participate in decision-making through decentralized mechanisms, gain access to future decentralized applications (dApps), and build long-term sustainable growth powered by open-market liquidity mechanics.
